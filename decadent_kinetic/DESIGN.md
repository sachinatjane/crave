## Brand & Style
The design system embodies an ultra-modern, hyper-sensory food-tech visual language tailored for the high-velocity social mobile landscape (TikTok, Instagram Reels, Pinterest). It captures the visceral temptation of viral confectionery culture: glossy finishes, rich tactile textures, dynamic kinetic layouts, and punchy editorial impact.

The interface prioritizes quick-scan visual snacking: full-bleed food cinematography, pill-shaped metric badges, high-contrast action triggers, and thumb-driven navigation. The visual style merges modern editorial minimalism with subtle glassmorphic overlays and warm organic undertones, evoking the sensory decadence of warm ganache, whipped chantilly cream, and mirror glazes while retaining the speed, crispness, and utility of modern product design.

## Colors
The color architecture balances warm dessert-inspired neutrals with vibrant confectionery accents designed for maximum conversion and appetite appeal:

- **Primary (`#E11D48` — Raspberry Glaze):** The primary brand trigger. Used for critical interactive focal points: primary CTA buttons, active state indicators, high-engagement heart counters, and cooking timers.
- **Secondary (`#1E130E` — Dark Espresso Ganache):** Provides grounded, high-contrast anchor weights. Used for high-emphasis typography, prominent bottom sheets, full-contrast "Cook Mode" overlays, and high-impact structural borders.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` — Golden Honeycomb):** The accent highlight. Applied to ratings, trending metric badges, difficulty meters, and spotlight badges.
- **Supporting Accents (Functional Palette):**
  - Pistachio / Matcha (`#10B981` / `#059669`): Dietary badges (vegan, gluten-free), ingredient check states, and success notifications.
  - Sweet Strawberry (`#FF385C`): Social share spikes, live view counter glows, and viral rank badges.
- **Backgrounds & Canvas Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas uses warm Custard Cream (`#FFFDF9`) and Vanilla Whey (`#FBF6EF`) rather than sterile blue-grays, providing an inviting, tactile backdrop that flatters culinary photography.
  - Dark surfaces for media viewing and overlay trays leverage deep Cocoa bean (`#140D0A`) to keep ambient light low and food colors brilliant.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans delivers geometric precision with sculpted, friendly apertures. It yields high editorial punch in display weights while preserving crystalline legibility in fast-scroll mobile viewports.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set with tight tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) and bold/extra-bold weights to mirror contemporary foodie editorial culture.
- **Body:** Calibrated line heights (1.45–1.5x) ensure quick skimming during live kitchen prep. Numbers and measurements feature tabular lining metrics for aligned ingredient tables.
- **Labels & Badges:** Uppercase, tightened, and high-weight (700/800) for social metrics, viral tags, preparation times, and difficulty pills.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 4-column layout governs mobile phones (320px–599px), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets (600px–1023px) and a centralized 12-column max-width (1200px) system on desktop. 

- **Thumb-Zone Optimization:** Bottom edge margins reserve an 88px clearance for the sticky floating CTA bar ("Cook Mode" & Pinterest Save).
- **Edge-to-Edge Media Rails:** Hero dessert reels and step-by-step videos extend flush to screen borders (`margin: 0`), while nested content cards lock to the standard `1rem` margin.
- **Micro-Gaps:** Compact ingredient listings use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for high-density scanning; generous `space-xl` dividers mark macro recipe milestones (Prep, Bake, Glaze, Chill).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is rendered through translucent frosted surfaces, warm tinted ambient glows, and dark chocolate contrast boundaries rather than harsh, desaturated drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Custard Cream (`#FFFDF9`), unbordered, zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Vanilla Whey (`#FBF6EF`) or Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm, diffuse ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 19, 14, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Floating Badges & Pills):** Frosted glass surfaces (`rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) bordered by a subtle 1px inner rim (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) and cast shadow: `box-shadow: 0 6px 24px -4px rgba(30, 19, 14, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Navigation Bars):** Deep Espresso surfaces or elevated pure white cards with dual-shadow layering: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -6px rgba(30, 19, 14, 0.18), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.08)`.
- **Level 4 (Cook Mode Fullscreen Sheet):** High-density backdrop overlay: `rgba(20, 13, 10, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`.

## Shapes
The design system adopts a pill-shaped aesthetic (`roundedness: 3`) to match the soft, organic, indulgent forms of pastries, glazes, and confectionery drops:

- **Pills & Chips (Full Radius `9999px`):** Viral metrics, cooking durations, difficulty indicators, serving counters, and filter tags.
- **Recipe Cards & Video Containers (`rounded-xl` / `24px`):** Large interactive cards mimic soft modern viewfinders.
- **Inner Micro-Elements (`rounded-md` / `12px`):** Checkboxes, ingredient thumbnail frames, and thumbnail badges maintain harmonized, softened internal geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Start Cooking", "Make This"):** High-contrast Raspberry Glaze (`#E11D48`) fill with bold white typography, pill-shaped (`9999px`), 52px touch height, with a dynamic active press scale (`0.97`).
- **Cook Mode Trigger:** High-contrast Dark Espresso (`#1E130E`) surface with gold honey accent accents, white bold typography, floating above the mobile bottom safe zone with a persistent timer glyph.
- **Social Action Buttons (Pinterest Save, Share, Like):** Translucent backdrop-blur circular or pill icons (`rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.9)`) with 1px border (`rgba(30, 19, 14, 0.08)`) and high-impact strawberry active states.

### Chips & Viral Tags
- **TikTok/Reel Metric Badges:** Tiny pill-shaped badges overlaid directly onto video frames. Jet Espresso or ultra-frosted background with warm white text, pairing an energetic spark icon with micro view counters (`1.4M views`).
- **Flavor & Diet Tags:** Subtle tinted backgrounds (e.g., pistachio tint `#E8F8F2` with dark forest text `#059669`) with `label-md` weight.

### Recipe Scaling & Serving Toggles
- **Stepper Segmented Control:** Full-pill shaped slider container (`#FBF6EF`) containing minus, count, and plus touch segments. Provides immediate visual and numeric recalculation of all ingredient volumes.

### Checklists (Step-by-Step Ingredients)
- **Interactive Check Rows:** 48px minimum vertical touch target. Tapping triggers a micro-bounce animation, strikes through the ingredient title in warm muted cocoa, and fills the circular check indicator with Pistachio Green (`#10B981`).

### Cards & Media Viewports
- **Social Recipe Preview Card:** Vertical aspect ratio (4:5 or 9:16 optimized for mobile viewports). Media takes priority with gradient scrim at bottom edge for text readability. Bottom contains title, creator avatar pill, preparation duration, and rating score.

### Floating Action HUD
- **Persistent Bottom Bar:** Floating 16px above screen bottom margin, housing the dynamic Cook Mode split button (`80%` width) paired with a high-contrast Pinterest Save icon (`20%` width).
