Step-by-Step Guide

Tap any step timer to launch Cook Mode right there

restaurant_menu
1

Line Freezer Tray & Prep Mix-Ins

Line a rimmed baking sheet or loaf pan with parchment paper so your frozen treat lifts out cleanly. Measure out 2 cups thick plain greek yogurt and 2 tbsp pure raw honey or maple syrup and keep toppings like 3/4 cup sliced strawberries, blueberries & pistachios prepped within reach.

2

Spread Thin & Snap Into Bark Shards

Whisk 2 cups thick plain greek yogurt with 2 tbsp pure raw honey or maple syrup, spread onto parchment-lined tray 1/3-inch thick, scatter 3/4 cup sliced strawberries, blueberries & pistachios, and freeze 2 hours before snapping into shards.

lightbulb
Creator Secret

Use full-fat or 2% strained Greek yogurt so the bark stays creamy instead of icy.

3

Transfer, Smooth Top & Add Garnish

Pour or press the mixture evenly into your prepared dish, smoothing the surface with a spatula. Scatter 3/4 cup sliced strawberries, blueberries & pistachios evenly across the top and press gently so it adheres cleanly.

4

Freeze Solid, Portion & Enjoy

Freeze Frozen Greek Yogurt Berry Crunch Bark flat until completely solid, then let sit 3 minutes at room temperature before slicing, snapping, or scooping into 6 servings. Store leftovers in a freezer-safe container.

Nutrition, Storage & Healthy Prep Notes

CALORIES135 kcal
SERVINGS6
SUGAR MODEZero Refined

Make-Ahead & Storage: Keep frozen greek yogurt berry crunch bark in an airtight glass container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days (or freeze individual portions up to 1 month). Pair with unsweetened Greek yogurt, fresh berries, or espresso for a balanced treat.

Keto & Low-Sugar Tip: Toggle Healthy Swap above to substitute refined sugar with monk fruit or coconut sugar and white flour with oat or almond flour.
