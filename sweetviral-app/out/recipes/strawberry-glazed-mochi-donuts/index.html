Step-by-Step Guide

Tap any step timer to launch Cook Mode right there

restaurant_menu
1

Preheat Air Fryer & Measure Ingredients

Preheat your air fryer to 330°F (165°C) for 3 minutes and line the basket with perforated parchment or a light oil mist. Measure out 1 1/2 cups glutinous sweet rice flour (mochiko), 3/4 cup silken tofu or warm coconut milk, and 3 tbsp granulated cane sugar in a mixing bowl so they combine evenly.

2

Knead Bouncy Mochi Balls

Mix 1 1/2 cups glutinous sweet rice flour (mochiko), 3 tbsp granulated cane sugar, and 3/4 cup silken tofu or warm coconut milk until a smooth play-dough texture forms. Roll into 48 mini spheres and connect 8 spheres per ring on parchment squares.

3

Air Fry or Flash Fry Golden

Mist lightly with oil and air-fry at 360°F (180°C) for 8 minutes (or fry 2 mins per side) until puffed and crisp outside. Work in small batches so the oil temperature stays steady, frying for 2–3 minutes per side until deeply golden and crisp all around.

lightbulb
Creator Secret

Leaving the parchment square underneath makes transferring delicate rings effortless—peel paper away after 60 seconds.

4

Dip in Strawberry Mirror Glaze

Whisk 1 cup powdered icing sugar, 2 tbsp crushed freeze-dried strawberries, and 2 tbsp warm milk until silky. Dip warm donut tops and finish with crisp sugar pearls. Gently press the coating onto every side so it adheres evenly, then let excess drip off before transferring to a clean tray.

Nutrition, Storage & Healthy Prep Notes

CALORIES240 kcal
SERVINGS6
SUGAR MODEClassic

Make-Ahead & Storage: Keep strawberry glazed mochi donuts in an airtight glass container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days (or freeze individual portions up to 1 month). Pair with unsweetened Greek yogurt, fresh berries, or espresso for a balanced treat.

Keto & Low-Sugar Tip: Toggle Healthy Swap above to substitute refined sugar with monk fruit or coconut sugar and white flour with oat or almond flour.
