Step-by-Step Guide

Tap any step timer to launch Cook Mode right there

restaurant_menu
1

Preheat Oven & Prep Your Pan

Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line your baking pan with parchment paper, leaving a slight overhang for effortless lifting. Measure out 1/2 cup softened butter or creamy nut butter and 3/4 cup brown sugar & granulated sugar so everything is ready and at room temperature for a silky, lump-free batter.

2

Cream Butter-Sugar & Fold Mix-Ins

Cream 1/2 cup softened butter or creamy nut butter and 3/4 cup brown sugar & granulated sugar fluffy (2 mins), stir in 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour + 1/2 tsp baking soda and 3/4 cup pure madagascar vanilla bean paste. Line your baking pan with parchment paper or grease lightly, and make sure 1/2 cup softened butter or creamy nut butter, 3/4 cup brown sugar & granulated sugar, and 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour + 1/2 tsp baking soda are measured and at room temperature so they blend smoothly.

3

Bake Golden Edges & Soft Centers

Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 11 minutes until edges turn golden while centers look slightly soft. Let Sugar cookie cool for 10–15 minutes so the crumb sets cleanly before slicing into 12 portions, then garnish and serve.

lightbulb
Creator Secret

Let the tray rest 5 minutes on the counter after baking so edges crisp while centers stay bakery-soft.

ac_unit

Cool on the warm sheet for 5 minutes so cookies set into chewy perfection.

4

Cool Completely, Garnish & Serve

Transfer Sugar cookie to a wire cooling rack and let it cool for 15–20 minutes so the interior sets cleanly without crumbling. Lift out using the parchment overhang, garnish as desired, and slice into 12 portions.

Nutrition, Storage & Healthy Prep Notes

Make-Ahead & Storage: Keep sugar cookie in an airtight glass container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days (or freeze individual portions up to 1 month). Pair with unsweetened Greek yogurt, fresh berries, or espresso for a balanced treat.

Keto & Low-Sugar Tip: Toggle Healthy Swap above to substitute refined sugar with monk fruit or coconut sugar and white flour with oat or almond flour.
