Step-by-Step Guide

Tap any step timer to launch Cook Mode right there

restaurant_menu
1

Whisk & Combine Base Ingredients

Blend all the mousse ingredients together in your food processor until smooth. Add the cacao powder first and, as you blend, have all the ingredients to hand in order to adjust the ratios slightly as the size of avocados and bananas varies so much. The perfect ratio in order to avoid the dish tasting too much of either is to use equal amounts of both. Incorporate 1 whole banana, 3 tbsp cacao, 1 whole avocado, and 2 tbsp honey smoothly until evenly distributed.

lightbulb
Creator Secret

Prep and measure banana first—mise en place makes this dessert twice as fast.

2

Fold In Flavorings & Mix-Ins

Taste and add a few drops of stevia if you feel you need more sweetness. Combine until completely smooth, uniform, and free of lumps before moving to the next stage. Incorporate 1 tsp lemon juice, 1 tsp vanilla, 2 tbsp water, and 1 pinch sea salt smoothly until evenly distributed.

3

Chill Until Firmly Set

Fill little cups or shot glasses with the mousse, sprinkle with the cacao powder or nibs and serve. Chill or freeze until completely firm and set, then portion into 4 servings and store leftovers in an airtight container.

4

Freeze Solid, Slice & Serve

Tip If you don’t have a frozen banana to hand you can just use a normal one and then chill the mousse before serving for a cooling dessert.

lightbulb
Creator Secret

Add a pinch of flaky sea salt right as it leaves the oven—it makes the dark chocolate flavor taste 2x richer.

ac_unit

Let set for 5–10 minutes before slicing or plating so the textures hold crisp and photogenic!

Make-Ahead & Storage: Keep chocolate avocado mousse in an airtight glass container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days (or freeze individual portions up to 1 month). Pair with unsweetened Greek yogurt, fresh berries, or espresso for a balanced treat.

Keto & Low-Sugar Tip: Toggle Healthy Swap above to substitute refined sugar with monk fruit or coconut sugar and white flour with oat or almond flour.
