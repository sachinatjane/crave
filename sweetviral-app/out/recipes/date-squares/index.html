Step-by-Step Guide

Tap any step timer to launch Cook Mode right there

restaurant_menu
1

Prep & Mix: Date squares

Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Grease an 8-inch (20 cm) square baking pan. Line your baking pan with parchment paper or grease lightly, and make sure 2 1/2 cups pitted dates, 1 cup water, 1 1/2 cups vanilla, and 2 cups oats are measured and at room temperature so they blend smoothly.

lightbulb
Creator Secret

Prep and measure pitted dates first—mise en place makes this dessert twice as fast.

2

Build Flavor: Date squares

To make the date filing: Combine the dates and water in a medium saucepan over high heat and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to low and simmer gently, stirring occasionally until the mixture thickens, around 6 to 8 minutes. Remove from heat, stir in vanilla and let cool. Set aside. Incorporate 1 cup all purpose flour, 1 cup brown sugar, 1/2 tsp baking powder, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1 cup unsalted butter smoothly until evenly distributed.

3

Shape & Layer: Date squares

Combine rolled oats, flour, sugar, baking soda and salt in a food processor. Pulse 3 times (careful not to overmix, you don’t want the mixture to be powdery, just combined). Add butter and pulse again until the mixture is crumbly in pea-sized clumps. Transfer to a mixing bowl to thoroughly mix in the butter.

4

Bake / Chill: Date squares

Spread half of the oat mixture on the bottom of the prepared baking pan, pressing down to make a flat, equal bottom later. Add the date mixture over the crust and then spread evenly. Layer with the remaining oat mixture and press down to flatten the top layer.

5

Viral Finish: Date squares

Bake in the centre of the oven until the top is golden brown, 30 to 35 minutes. Let cool completely in the pan before cutting and serving. Date Squares keep well, covered in the refrigerator for up to a week, and frozen for 2 to 3 months.

lightbulb
Creator Secret

Measure aromatics and syrups before heating so timing stays crisp and your glaze hits glossy perfection.

ac_unit

Let set for 5–10 minutes before slicing or plating so the textures hold crisp and photogenic!

Make-Ahead & Storage: Keep date squares in an airtight glass container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days (or freeze individual portions up to 1 month). Pair with unsweetened Greek yogurt, fresh berries, or espresso for a balanced treat.

Keto & Low-Sugar Tip: Toggle Healthy Swap above to substitute refined sugar with monk fruit or coconut sugar and white flour with oat or almond flour.
