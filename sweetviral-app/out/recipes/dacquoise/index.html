Step-by-Step Guide

Tap any step timer to launch Cook Mode right there

restaurant_menu
1

Preheat Oven & Prep Your Pan

Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line your baking pan with parchment paper, leaving a slight overhang for effortless lifting. Measure out 1 3/4 cups cake or all-purpose flour + baking powder, 1 cup granulated or brown sugar, and 1/2 cup softened butter or neutral oil so everything is ready and at room temperature for a silky, lump-free batter.

2

Mix Batter & Bake Until Springy

Cream 1/2 cup softened butter or neutral oil and 1 cup granulated or brown sugar, beat in 3 eggs large eggs + 3/4 cup buttermilk, and fold in 1 3/4 cups cake or all-purpose flour + baking powder. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 30 mins until a toothpick comes out clean.

3

Bake Dacquoise & Check Doneness

Bake for 30 minutes until the edges turn golden and set while the center remains tender. Test with a toothpick—if it comes out with a few moist crumbs rather than wet batter, it is ready.

4

Cool Completely & Crown with Filling

Cool sponge 20 minutes, then layer or glaze generously with 1 cup toasted chopped pecans, almonds, or hazelnuts. Let Dacquoise cool for 10–15 minutes so the crumb sets cleanly before slicing into 10 portions, then garnish and serve.

Creator Secret

Frosting a warm cake melts buttercream—wait until the pan feels cool to the touch.

Make-Ahead & Storage: Keep dacquoise in an airtight glass container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days (or freeze individual portions up to 1 month). Pair with unsweetened Greek yogurt, fresh berries, or espresso for a balanced treat.

Keto & Low-Sugar Tip: Toggle Healthy Swap above to substitute refined sugar with monk fruit or coconut sugar and white flour with oat or almond flour.
