Step-by-Step Guide

Tap any step timer to launch Cook Mode right there

restaurant_menu
1

Prep & Mix: Jam Roly-Poly

Put a deep roasting tin onto the bottom shelf of the oven, and make sure that there’s another shelf directly above it. Pull the roasting tin out on its shelf, fill two-thirds with boiling water from the kettle, then carefully slide it back in. Heat oven to 180C/160C fan/gas 4. Tear off a large sheet of foil and greaseproof paper (about 30 x 40cm). Sit the greaseproof on top of the foil and butter it. Incorporate 3 1/3 tbsp butter, 1 1/8 cup self-raising flour, 1 pc vanilla, and 3 1/3 tbsp suet smoothly until evenly distributed.

lightbulb
Creator Secret

Prep and measure butter first—mise en place makes this dessert twice as fast.

2

Build Flavor: Jam Roly-Poly

Tip butter, flour and vanilla seeds into a food processor; pulse until the butter has disappeared. Tip into a mixing bowl. Stir through the suet, pour in the milk and work together with a cutlery knife until you get a sticky dough. You may need a drop more milk, depending on your flour. Incorporate 5/8 cup milk, 6 2/3 tbsp raspberry jam, and 1 to serve custard smoothly until evenly distributed.

3

Shape & Layer: Jam Roly-Poly

Tip the dough out onto a floured surface, quickly pat together to smooth, then roll out to a square roughly 25 x 25cm. Spread the jam all over, leaving a gap along one edge, then roll up from the opposite edge. Pinch the jam-free edge into the dough where it meets, and pinch the ends roughly, too. Carefully lift onto the greased paper, join-side down (you might find a flat baking sheet helpful for this), loosely bring up the paper and foil around it, then scrunch together along the edges and ends to seal. The roly-poly will puff quite a bit during cooking so don’t wrap it tightly. Lift the parcel directly onto the rack above the tin and cook for 1 hr.

4

Bake / Chill: Jam Roly-Poly

Let the pudding sit for 5 mins before unwrapping, then carefully open the foil and paper, and thickly slice to serve. Plate into 4 generous servings while the flavors and textures are at their peak, and store any leftovers covered.

Make-Ahead & Storage: Keep jam roly-poly in an airtight glass container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days (or freeze individual portions up to 1 month). Pair with unsweetened Greek yogurt, fresh berries, or espresso for a balanced treat.
