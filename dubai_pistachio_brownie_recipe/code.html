Step-by-Step Guide

Follow precisely for peak ASMR crunch

restaurant_menu
1

Crisp the Kataifi Crunch

5 Mins

Chop kataifi strands into 1-inch lengths. Melt butter over medium heat in a wide skillet, add noodles, and stir constantly until uniformly toasted to a rich amber golden hue. Cool slightly.

2

Mix Pistachio Velvet

3 Mins

In a mixing bowl, blend the sweetened pistachio butter and sesame tahini until ultra-smooth. Gently fold in the toasted kataifi noodles until every crunchy shard is coated in emerald paste.

lightbulb
Creator Secret

Don't skip the tahini! Its roasted nuttiness balances the sweet pistachio paste and replicates the original Dubai chocolate bar mouthfeel.

3

The Fudgy Pour & Stuffing

7 Mins

Whip brownie batter until glossy. Pour 55% into a parchment-lined 8x8 pan. Smooth with spatula. Spread the thick pistachio kataifi layer edge-to-edge. Ladle remaining brownie batter over top.

4

Bake & The Viral Cut

25 Mins

Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 25 mins until edges set and center has a gentle fudge wobble.

ac_unit

CRITICAL CUT TIP: Chill in fridge for 20 mins before cutting for the famous viral cross-section clean line & crisp ASMR crunch sound.
